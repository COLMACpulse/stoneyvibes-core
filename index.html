ABOUT

  • StoneyVibes is a private session capture tool. It helps you observe how different cannabis samples actually land for you — not what the label says, and not what someone else claims.
  • It can capture: the sample, sleep quality, mood before, visual traits, smell notes, terpene presence by perception, voice notes, optional photos, and a simple “run it again?” rating.
  • Over time it becomes useful as a personal reference library. Patterns start to appear: what helps sleep, what sharpens thinking, what feels social, what feels heavy, and what you would rather skip.
  • Everything stays local on this device unless you export it. Nothing is shared unless you choose to move it.
  • Use it however it serves you best: as a logbook, a smell notebook, a personal cannabis map, or just a memory aid.
