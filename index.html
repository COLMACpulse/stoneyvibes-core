About & settings

What this is

Lifted States is a private observation instrument for experienced cannabis users. It helps you notice how different samples actually land for you — not what the label claims, not what anyone else says.

The rule of the system: a signal is only real if it survives time and repeats across different conditions. One strong session is a story. A signal is a story that keeps coming back.

Data & privacy

  • Everything stays on this device.
  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Photos and audio live in this browser's local storage.
  • Export gives you a JSON file you fully own.
  • Wipe removes everything from this device with no remainder.
