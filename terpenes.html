WHAT THEY TEND TO FEEL LIKE

These are common associations—use them as a compass, not gospel.

Myrcene

Often: heavy body, couchy, earthy/musky, “blanket” feel.

Limonene

Often: lift / brightness, citrus sharpness, mood-up, clean edge.

β-Caryophyllene

Often: pepper/spice bite, warm “pressure”, grounded intensity.

α/β-Pinene

Often: pine/forest, alert clarity, “sharp air”.

Linalool

Often: soft floral, calm / soothing, gentler landing.

Terpinolene

Often: sweet/herbal, airy head feel, “sparkly” top note.
